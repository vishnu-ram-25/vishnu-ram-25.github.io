One deployment pipeline for every app

Each repository had its own copy of the build-and-deploy steps, and they drifted apart.

  • A shared, versioned GitHub Actions workflow: build, push to ECR, roll out to EKS
  • Apps opt in with a few lines and upgrade by changing one version tag
  • Standard probes and rollout defaults for every new app

Self-hosted monitoring on Kubernetes

Vulnerability scanning in CI and in the cluster

Nobody knew which running images carried critical CVEs or baked-in secrets.

  • A Trivy gate that blocks fixable CRITICAL and HIGH findings
  • Daily rescans of running workloads, with a dashboard and alerts
  • A remediation policy with fix deadlines

Backups and cross-region disaster recovery

The business needed to know how long a full region outage would take to recover from.

  • Velero backups of cluster workloads, with a restore actually tested
  • A cross-region DR plan in Terraform with database replicas
  • Failover runbooks and costs for each recovery tier
